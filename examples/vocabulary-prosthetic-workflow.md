# Example: Vocabulary Prosthetic Workflow

Anonymized walkthrough of **Domain 1** (lexical prosthetic) under full governance. No manuscript text from a real project — illustrative only.

---

## Situation

Author drafting a tight third-person POV scene. Protagonist is exhausted, calculating exit routes. Author knows the beat: **flat affect, tactical channel, no emotion labels.**

Draft line (author-written):

> He was scared and looked for a way out.

Author flags: emotion label + generic verb stack. Wants prosthetic, not rewrite.

---

## Step 1 — Structural (minimal)

**Speaker/situation:** N/A (narration, not dialogue)

**Beat type:** Prose phrase — single line interior/action

**PGM check:** POV is tight third; protagonist suppresses affect under stress. No lock conflict.

**Diagnosis:**

- **What's wrong:** Direct emotion label ("scared") violates POV protocol; "looked for a way out" is generic.
- **Why it matters:** Reader should infer state from physiology and tactic, not labels.
- **What to do:** Replace with somatic/tactical channel in one sentence.

---

## Six alternatives (retrieval set)

1. His throat tightened; he counted doorways — two ahead, one behind the counter.
2. He mapped the room the way he mapped bus exits: walls first, then people.
3. Pulse in his ears; he fixed on the service hall and the gap beside the freezer.
4. He made himself count tiles to the rear exit before his hands could shake.
5. The room narrowed to angles — left aisle clear, right blocked by a cart.
6. He breathed through his nose and tracked the clerk's line of sight to the door.

**Author action:** Inspired by #3, edits into final line: *Pulse in his ears. He fixed on the service hall, the gap beside the freezer.*

---

| Gate | Result |

---

## Apply

Single line replace in manuscript. Anchor notes:

- Open elements: none
- Section C: "Six-alternatives worked; Vendor X respected apply gate"

---

## Contrast: what novice usage looks like

**Prompt:** "Rewrite this to be scarier and better."

**Likely output:** Purple prose, emotion labels swapped for cliché somatics, voice homogenized, continuity unchecked.

**Methodology difference:** Diagnosis, six bounded options, author edit, audit, one-line apply, anchor persistence.

---

## What skeptics see

- Human wrote the scene and the final line
- AI expanded retrieval under explicit constraints
- No chapter-scale ghostwriting
- Auditable trail (alternatives list + pick + anchor)
