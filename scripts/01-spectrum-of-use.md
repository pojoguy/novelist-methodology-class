## Hook (0:00–0:05)

> "Write me a bestseller" is level zero. Serious authors operate at level two through seven.

**On-screen:** "Level 0 → Level 7"

---

## Beat 1 — Level 0 vs everything else (0:05–0:45)

**Say:**

> Level zero is ghostwriting: accept the output, call it yours. Levels one through seven are different tools — brainstorming, vocabulary prosthetics, developmental audit, continuity graphs, grounding sandboxes, production blueprints, agentic workflows with gates. Most public debate is about level zero. This series is about the rest.

**On-screen:** Spectrum ladder graphic (0–7)

---

## Beat 2 — Lexical prosthetic (0:45–1:30)

**Say:**

> Level two: you know the beat, you can't retrieve the word. The model offers six alternatives — inspiration, not a menu order. You pick, blend, reject, or write your own. That's not ghostwriting — that's external working memory. Same move as tapping an autocomplete suggestion.

**On-screen:** `6 alternatives → you decide`

---

## Beat 3 — Audit and state (1:30–2:30)

**Say:**

> Level three: adversarial editorial pass — no apply without your instruction. Level four: character sheets and timeline graphs the model must read before it speaks. Level five: pressure-test beats against real geography and period detail. Chat memory is not canon. Registered state is.

**On-screen:** Audit / PGM / Grounding icons

---

## Beat 4 — Talking to skeptics (2:30–3:15)

**Say:**

> When someone says AI can't write real books — agree, if they mean level zero. When they say any AI use is cheating — ask which level. Lexical prosthetic is closer to spell-check and a brutal beta reader than to outsourcing your novel.

**On-screen:** "Which level?"
