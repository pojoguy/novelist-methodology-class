## Demo environment — locked

| Role | Tool | Rule |
|------|------|------|
| **Level 0 (this video)** | **Frontier LLM in a blank chat UI** — ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini, etc. | **No** project rules, **no** repo, **no** custom instructions, **no** Cursor |
| **Levels 2–7 (later episodes)** | Cursor + templates + PGMs + apply gates | **Not** used in this recording |

**Why not Cursor for Level 0:** This installation loads prosthetic rules, apply gates, and six-alternative protocol at launch. That is **Level 7 infrastructure** — it cannot demonstrate unconstrained ghostwriting honestly.

**Disclosure (say once on camera or VO):** *"Staged demo on synthetic prose in a blank chat — not my manuscript workflow."*

**Sample line only — do not use real manuscript text:**

```text

```

### BEAT 1 — Setup `[SCREEN]` (0:20–0:50)

**Show:** Empty chat thread. Paste sample line.

**Say (VO):**

> One sentence. Emotion label, generic exit — deliberately weak. No character bible, no voice rules, no apply gate. This is the baseline.

**On-screen:** `Synthetic sample · not a real draft`

**Locked prompt 0 (paste only the sample line — no instruction yet):**

```text
He felt afraid and looked for a way out.
```

---

### BEAT 2 — "Make it better" `[SCREEN]` (0:50–1:50)

**Show:** Type prompt → wait for full reply → **do not edit** → leave output on screen.

**Say (VO):**

> Most novices don't ask for six alternatives. They ask for a rewrite and take the first answer.

**Locked prompt 1:**

```text
Rewrite this paragraph to be more emotional and literary.
```

**On-screen:** `Prompt 1 · one-shot rewrite`

**Point out on VO (after output lands):** telling ("felt afraid"), goldilocks cadence, generic imagery — **diagnose, don't nitpick every line**.

---

### BEAT 3 — "Write my chapter" `[SCREEN]` (1:50–2:50)

**Show:** New message in **same** thread **or** fresh chat (either is realistic). No story bible pasted.

**Locked prompt 2:**

```text
Write chapter 3 of my YA fantasy novel. Make it exciting.
```

**Say (VO):**

> No canon file. No POV lock. The model invents — plausible, average, disposable. That's not craft infrastructure; that's outsourcing.

**On-screen:** `No PGM · no canon · no gates`

---

### BEAT 4 — "Fix my chapter" `[SCREEN]` (2:50–3:40)

**Show:** Paste 2–3 sentences of **obviously weak** synthetic prose (can extend sample line into a short paragraph you write in prep). Single "fix" prompt.

**Locked prompt 3:**

```text
Fix my chapter. Make it publishable.
```

**Say (VO):**

> "Fix" becomes **substitution**. The model chooses; you didn't branch a retrieval set. No audit. No numbered recommendations. This is ghostwrite drift in one prompt.

**On-screen:** `Model chose · you didn't`

---

### BEAT 5 — Contrast + verdict `[CAM]` or `[SCREEN]` stills (3:40–4:30)

**Show:** Split still or cut between Level 0 output and a **single human line** you speak (improvised or prep):

```text
His throat tightened. He counted doorways — two ahead, one behind the counter.
```

**Say:**

> Same tool family. Different **contract**. Level zero: human prose mass near zero. Level two and up: you constrain, you select, you own the file. That's what this series documents — not this.

**On-screen:** `L0: accept` | `L2+: you decide`
