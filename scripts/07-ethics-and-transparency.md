## Beat 1 — Specific labels (0:05–1:15)

**Say:**

> Ideation only. Lexical prosthetic — AI offered words, I selected all lines. Developmental audit — AI critiqued, I revised. Continuity tooling — AI queried my database, prose is mine. Ghostwriting — say so if it's true. This repo advocates high human prose mass with bounded assistance.

**On-screen:** Label list

---

## Beat 2 — Authorship (1:15–2:00)

**Say:**

> Author means intent, voice, selection, responsibility. AI means retrieval set, audit report, graph query. If you accept model paragraphs without substantial rewrite, your disclosure should reflect that — regardless of what you intended.

**On-screen:** Author vs AI roles

---

## Beat 3 — What we refuse (2:00–2:45)

**Say:**

> We don't normalize publishing unedited model output as craft, hiding AI use when required, or replacing paid sensitivity readers with unchecked models. Disability framing matters too — lexical prosthetic can be accessibility, not cheating.
