## Pedagogy to honor on camera (from paired doc)

When recording Levels **1–3** in frontier chat, **do not** rely on a "brutal editor" store persona — use the **prompts in each level script**: word contracts, readonly audit, [`editorial-recommendations`](../templates/editorial-recommendations.md) letter shape. Optional one-liner on camera:

> "Chat flatters by default. I paste the letter template and spell out what *priority fixes* means — not a custom GPT that calls itself brutal."

**Scale (maintainer experience — cite if a demo runs long):**

- About **one governed chapter per sitting** in frontier chat is a honest ceiling.  
- A **half-page** resolving **several long-running arcs** can bog down sooner than a full single-arc chapter.  
- Levels **4+** demos assume **PGM files you reload** — same habit as drafting PGMs in chat, then saving ([`docs/01` — *Building PGMs*](../docs/01-spectrum-of-use.md#building-pgms-in-frontier-chat)).
