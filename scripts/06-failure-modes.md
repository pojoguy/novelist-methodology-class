## Beat 1 — Sparse-edge collapse (0:05–0:40)

**Say:**

> Too many constraints at once and the model collapses to "not A, not B, C" — negation triangulation. Wrong fix: add more rules. Right fix: diagnose the conflict, offer six alternatives, two-step pipeline with quote, tagline, and apply gates.

**On-screen:** "More rules ≠ fix"

---

## Beat 2 — Ghostwrite drift (0:40–1:05)

**Say:**

> Priority fixes becomes substituted prose — or you said "here's my take" and it applied the nearest numbered option. Audit, alternatives, stop — unless you name a specific line fix. Pick and cluster plus author take are not the same move.

**On-screen:** `Audit ≠ rewrite` · `Pick ≠ here's my take`

---

## Beat 3 — Six-alternative treadmill (1:05–1:25)

**Say:**

> Six alternatives plus convergence rounds and still nothing lands? That's usually a malformed beat — wrong order, missing situation, grounding mismatch. Stop shopping wordings. Rewrite the paragraph or reload grounding.

**On-screen:** `Six fail → malformed beat`

---

## Beat 4 — Place, sandbox, and index traps (1:25–2:15)

**Say:**

> Generic place hallucination: stock cemetery — walls, trees, Gothic mood — when your real site is bald prairie. File map and Street View together; load before you revise. POV-blind is valid: your notes hold the cemetery; the character never names it. Check constraints, not keywords.

> Maps versus play-pretend: grounding answers where; scenario simulation answers who under pressure — same exploration folder, different promote targets. Don't paste sandbox chat into the chapter.

> Edited the chapter but skipped reanchor? CH-seven line sixty-three now points at the wrong line. Re-index or cite a quoted snippet.

**On-screen:** `Map + SV` · `5a ≠ 5b` · `Reanchor after edits`

---

## Beat 5 — Structural traps (2:15–2:45)

**Say:**

> Proverb dialogue: six wise-worker lines, no speaker — quote gate first. Thesis taglines: freedom versus duty as craft feedback. Wrong causal merge: two lore facts collapsed — run a continuity check with a reference ID, not chat vibes. Mixed lookup: one search kind per question.

**On-screen:** Trap examples (strikethrough style)
