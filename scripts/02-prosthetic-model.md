## Beat 1 — The contract (0:05–1:00)

**Say:**

> Prosthetic: expand your retrieval set, you choose. Ghostwriting: model chooses, you accept. Default output is diagnostics and alternatives — not finished paragraphs. Creative intent and final prose mass stay human.

**On-screen:** Side-by-side table (prosthetic vs ghostwrite)

---

## Beat 2 — Six alternatives (1:00–2:00)

**Say:**

> When constraints bind tight, the retrieval bias function hits a thin solution space — it has to return something but can't choose between near-misses. You get "not A, not B, but C," comma lists, adjective triads. That's not voice — that's forced emission. Six alternatives deliberately widen the space — inspiration, not a menu order. You pick, blend, reject, or write your own. You apply the final bias. The model doesn't pretend it chose.

**On-screen:** `Thin space → forced output` / `6 alternatives = widen space` / `You decide = final bias`

**Note for recording:** See `docs/02-prosthetic-model.md` — *Why six — theta bias rationale* for full technical depth.

## Beat 3 — Priority fixes gate (2:00–2:45)

**Say:**

> "Fix the priority issues" does not mean rewrite my chapter. It means complete the audit, branch alternatives, and stop. Author authorization to fix does not override the apply gate — unless you name a specific line, like "line forty-seven, add a period."

**On-screen:** "Priority fixes ≠ rewrite"

---

## Beat 4 — Two-step pipeline (2:45–3:45)

**Say:**

> For dialogue, grounding, and developmental edits: step one is structural — beats, questions, speaker and situation. Step two is readonly audit — quote gate, tagline gate, apply gate. Pass or fail. No rewrite on fail. This exists because the engine is stochastic — plausible, not true.

**On-screen:** Step 1 → Step 2 → Author pick → Apply
